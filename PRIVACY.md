# Calm Captions – Privacy Policy

Calm Captions does not collect, store, or send any personal data. It reads the subtitle text already shown on the page only to restyle it in your browser. Your style choices (font, size, colors) are saved with Chrome's built-in storage on your device only. Nothing is sent to any server, and no analytics or ads are used.

Permissions: "storage" saves your settings; site access to YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video lets the extension restyle captions on those pages.
