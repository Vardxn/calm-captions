# Calm Captions – Privacy Policy

Calm Captions does not collect, store, or send any personal data. It restyles the subtitles you already see on YouTube and Udemy, in your browser only.

- On YouTube it re-reads the caption file that the video player has already requested, from youtube.com itself, to follow the real caption timing. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- Your style choices (text size, background opacity, colors, on/off) are saved with Chrome's built-in storage on your device only.
- No analytics, no ads, no remote code.

Permissions: "storage" saves your settings; site access to YouTube and Udemy lets the extension restyle captions on those pages.
